# Seedance 2.5

Seedance 2.5 is an AI video generation model developed by **ByteDance**.

It can generate videos from text, images, video references, and audio references. One of its main strengths is creating longer, multi-scene videos while keeping characters, style, and motion more consistent.

## What Can Seedance 2.5 Do?

Seedance 2.5 supports several types of AI video generation:

- Text to Video
- Image to Video
- Video to Video
- Video Extension
- Video Editing
- Multi-scene Video Generation
- Audio and Video Generation
- Reference-based Video Generation

For example:

```text
Prompt:
A developer working late at night in a modern office,
cinematic lighting, camera slowly moving toward the monitor.
```

Seedance can turn this description into a generated video.

## Main Features

### Text to Video

You can describe a scene using a text prompt and Seedance will generate the video.

Example:

```text
A futuristic city at night with neon lights and flying cars,
cinematic camera movement.
```

---

### Image to Video

You can upload an image and animate it.

Example:

```text
Image:
A person standing in front of a building.

Instruction:
The person starts walking toward the camera while the camera slowly zooms out.
```

---

### Reference Support

Seedance can use reference files to control the generated result.

Supported references may include:

- Images
- Videos
- Audio

This can help maintain:

- Character appearance
- Clothing
- Environment
- Visual style
- Camera movement

---

## Video Length

Seedance 2.5 can generate videos up to approximately:

```text
30 seconds
```

This makes it useful for creating short cinematic sequences instead of only very short AI clips.

## Limitations

Like other AI video generators, Seedance may still have problems with:

- Complex hand movements
- Small text inside videos
- Exact logo reproduction
- Long character interactions
- Complex physics
- Maintaining perfect consistency across every frame

For professional projects, generated videos usually benefit from additional editing.
